Each channel is modelled as a Yule-Nielsen-style cube-root form, `XYZ = whitepoint * (polynomial in c, m, y)**3`, so the fitted polynomial lives in a space essentially linear in CIELAB, and the polynomial itself uses only exponents 1-3 per ink. Coefficients came from subset-selected least squares on the cube-root targets, then Levenberg-Marquardt refinement directly against CIEDE2000 (with a high-power objective to pull the worst patch down). On the 150 given pairs this gives **mean ΔE00 = 0.080, max ΔE00 = 0.216** (no patch above 0.22), and predicted XYZ stays positive and smooth across the whole 0-100 cmy cube.
